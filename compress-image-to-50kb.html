How it works

  1. Add a JPG, PNG, WebP or BMP image.
  2. 50KB opens as the target automatically.
  3. Squish lowers encoding quality first and only reduces dimensions if needed.
  4. Download the compressed image.

Why use a target instead of a quality slider?

A quality percentage does not tell you the final file size. A target-size workflow works backwards from the upload limit so you do not have to repeatedly export and check the result.

Privacy

This exact-size compressor runs in your browser. The image you select on the compressor page is not sent to the advanced resize API.
