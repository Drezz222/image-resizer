How to compress to 200KB

  1. Add your image.
  2. 200KB is loaded as the maximum output size.
  3. The compressor searches JPEG or WebP quality for the best fit.
  4. If necessary, it reduces pixel dimensions and tries again.

Useful for web uploads

200KB is large enough to keep many web images sharp while being much smaller than a typical phone photo. It can be a practical ceiling for profiles, thumbnails, forms and CMS uploads.

Privacy

The exact-size compressor runs in your browser rather than sending the selected image to the advanced resizing API.
