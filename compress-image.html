How exact-size compression works

Image encoders do not always produce one exact byte count, so Squish treats the number you enter as a maximum. It searches JPEG/WebP quality levels for the best-looking result under that limit. When even low quality is too large, it reduces the pixel dimensions and tries again.

Forms and applications

Useful for portals that reject profile photos, signatures or scans over 20KB, 50KB, 100KB or another fixed limit.

Privacy-friendly

The exact-size compressor uses your browser's Canvas API. The image selected on this page is not sent to the resize API.

Metadata removed

Re-encoding through canvas normally removes camera, GPS and other EXIF metadata embedded in the original image.
