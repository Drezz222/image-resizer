How to compress to 500KB

  1. Add a JPG, PNG, WebP or BMP image.
  2. 500KB is selected as the maximum file size.
  3. Squish searches for a high-quality encoded result under the target.
  4. Download the finished JPEG or WebP.

A good general-purpose target

500KB can dramatically reduce multi-megabyte camera images while retaining enough detail for many websites, documents and everyday sharing workflows.

Browser processing

The exact-size compressor works locally with the Canvas API. Your selected image is not sent to the server-side resize endpoint.
